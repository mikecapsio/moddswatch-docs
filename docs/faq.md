# MODDS.WATCH FAQ

Short answers about the product. The live page is https://modds.watch/faq. If a control in the lab disagrees with this file, trust the lab. If a legal question disagrees with this file, trust https://modds.watch/terms and https://modds.watch/privacy.

## Product

### What is MODDS.WATCH?

A free, browser-based 3D lab for planning watch mods. You preview colors, materials, straps, filters, and your own artwork on a supported watch before you buy parts or open the case. Photo Studio exports pictures of the result.

It is built for hobbyists, professional modders, and clients who need the same picture.

### Is it free?

Yes. The labs do not require a subscription, a trial, or a card. Optional support is $5/month on Patreon: https://www.patreon.com/cw/moddswatch/membership. Under the current Terms, that payment does not unlock features.

### Do I need an account?

No. The Terms say you can use the site without registering. There is nothing to sign into to open a lab.

### Who makes it?

Mike Caps, working solo as a developer, 3D artist, and watch modder. About: https://modds.watch/about. Contact: team@modds.watch.

### Is this AI, or an NFT site?

No. The preview is real-time 3D that responds to your settings. MODDS.WATCH does not generate the watch from a text prompt, and it does not sell digital collectibles.

### What is a mod watch?

A watch customized with swapped or aftermarket parts: dial, bezel, hands, strap, crystal, case finish, or a new digital faceplate. Modding is that hands-on hobby. Customization is the broader idea and also includes engraving, a logo, or a made-to-order piece. MODDS.WATCH covers the planning side of both.

Longer explainer: https://modds.watch/blog/what-is-a-mod-watch
Glossary: https://modds.watch/glossary

### Will you add Seiko or other brands?

Seiko is the most requested gap, and the site says so. SKX, Seiko 5, Turtle, and Samurai are covered in a written guide, not in a 3D lab. The FAQ also names Citizen, Invicta, Vostok, and Orient as later interests. Email team@modds.watch with requests. There is no vote button in the product.

### Can I upload my own 3D model?

No. You work on the watches the site has modeled.

### What can I change?

It depends on the model. Shared tools are orbit, lighting, background, Photo Studio, and Parts Finder. Digitals add case, strap, faceplate, color filters, and display treatments. The Duro adds bezel, hands, dial elements, and glass. Confirm on the open lab.

### Can I type my name onto the watch?

Put the name in an image and upload that image onto the faceplate. The AE-1200 Faceplate Text control recolors existing printed lettering. It does not typeset a new name.

### What image files work?

JPG, PNG, or WebP, up to 10 MB. The browser reads the file for the preview.

### Can I export the design?

Yes, as a picture from Photo Studio: Square or 16:9, Good or High quality. That is a render of the view, not a 3D file and not a parts spreadsheet.

## Buying, building, and accuracy

### Can I buy the watch or the parts here?

No. MODDS.WATCH does not sell watches, parts, or finished mods.

### Will it give me a shopping list?

No. A custom mix of colors and uploaded art does not map to one SKU. Parts Finder opens marketplace links for generic tools and parts. You, or your modder, confirm fit.

### Are Parts Finder links ads?

They go to Amazon or AliExpress. MODDS.WATCH may earn an affiliate commission if you buy. The lab discloses that. Price and availability are the marketplace's.

### Are the 3D models exact replicas?

No. The Terms describe them as artistic and educational representations. Use them to judge a look. Do not use them as manufacturing drawings, and do not expect a slider color to match a specific aftermarket coating.

### Does a preview mean the parts fit?

No. Lug width, module clearance, crystal size, and gaskets are physical checks.

### How much does a custom watch cost?

The site's public range is roughly $20–100 for many Casio builds, and often $150–400 or more for a Seiko mod with a quality dial, sapphire, and bezel. Custom printing and a hired modder cost more. Those are orientation numbers, not quotes.

### What tools do I need?

For many first mods: a spring bar tool, and if you open the watch, a case opener, a case press, tweezers, a loupe, and hand tools when you touch hands. Details: https://modds.watch/tools

## Saving, privacy, and sharing

### Are my designs saved?

Not in an account. Export a Photo Studio picture. If you leave the lab, treat the session as disposable. The app can warn you on the way out.

### Can a client and I edit the same design live?

No. Look at one screen together, or send the pictures. "Real time" describes the 3D view updating as you move a control.

### Who can see my upload?

The image is applied in your browser for the preview. MODDS.WATCH does not offer a public gallery of your session. If you post a Photo Studio picture, that post is public because you published it.

### Do you sell data?

No. The Privacy Policy says the site does not sell, rent, or trade user data. The site does use cookies and analytics, including Google Analytics, and it receives your email if you write in or support via Patreon.

### Who owns a design I make?

You do, under the Terms. If you share it publicly, you allow MODDS.WATCH to display it with credit as part of the community. Read the current Terms before you rely on that sentence: https://modds.watch/terms

## Practical

### Do I need a powerful computer?

You need a device that runs a current browser. People use phones and desktops. If a lab is heavy on an old phone, try a desktop browser.

# Watch Models in the Lab

MODDS.WATCH previews mods on specific watches. If a model is not listed on https://modds.watch/lab, you cannot open it in the builder yet.

The notes below match the labs as built. A control can move. If the lab screen and this page disagree, trust the lab.

## Casio F-91W

Lab: https://modds.watch/lab/casio/base/casio-f91w

Guide: https://modds.watch/blog/complete-guide-moddswatch-casio-f91w-modding-tools

The F-91W is a small, cheap, recognizable digital watch. Small changes read loudly because the case is small.

You can plan:

- Case color and material
- Three strap setups: the original F-91W strap, an A168 steel strap, and a NATO on 22 mm adapters
- Strap color, material, and an uploaded strap image
- Faceplate color, material, and an uploaded image
- Faceplate detail treatments: F-91W, F-91WB, and a cleaner layout
- LCD color filters, including gradient and an uploaded filter image
- Positive and negative display looks
- Lighting, background, stand, and Photo Studio

Parts Finder on this model points at the donor watch, metal cases, screen meshes, color filters, strap adapters, straps, protection, and basic tools.

A custom name or logo is an uploaded faceplate image, not typed text.

## Casio A168

Lab: https://modds.watch/lab/casio/base/casio-a168

Guide: https://modds.watch/blog/complete-guide-moddswatch-casio-a168-modding-tools

The A168 is the compact steel-bracelet digital. People use it for a stealth blackout, a warm vintage gold direction, a two-tone daily watch, or a custom face.

You can plan:

- Case color and material
- Original steel strap or NATO with adapters
- Strap color, material, and an uploaded image
- Faceplate color, material, and an uploaded image
- LCD color filters
- Positive and negative display
- Clean Mode, which strips the presentation back for a stealth read
- Lighting, background, stand, and Photo Studio

Parts Finder points at the donor watch, metal cases, color filters, strap adapters, straps, protection, and tools.

## Casio AE-1200 Royale

Lab: https://modds.watch/lab/casio/base/casio-ae1200

Guide: https://modds.watch/blog/complete-guide-moddswatch-casio-ae1200-modding-tools

The AE-1200 is the wide world-time watch often called the Casio Royale. The large faceplate is why people mod it: tactical, sci-fi, a custom map, or a metal-kit direction.

You can plan:

- Case color and material
- Steel bracelet or NATO 22 mm
- A control to match the strap finish to the case
- Strap color, material, and an uploaded image on the NATO
- Faceplate color, material, and an uploaded image
- Faceplate Text: color and finish of the lettering already printed on the face
- Bumper color and material
- Several LCD color filters, with color, transparency, gradient, or an image
- Display condition, including positive, negative, and split treatments
- Clean Mode
- Lighting, background, stand, and Photo Studio

Parts Finder points at the donor watch, metal case kits, faceplates, color filters, strap adapters, straps, protection, bumpers, and tools.

This is the strongest lab for a custom printed face, because the face is large enough for a map, a logo, or a simple graphic to read.

## Casio Duro MDV-106

Lab: https://modds.watch/lab/casio/duro/casio-duro-mdv106-1a

Guide: https://modds.watch/blog/casio-duro-modifications-guide

The Duro (the MDV-106 "Marlin," often discussed alongside the MDV-107) is a budget dive watch. Mods here are bezel, dial, hands, crystal, and strap, not an LCD faceplate.

You can plan:

- Glass look
- A color filter, including an uploaded image
- Bezel body, insert, and insert text, including an uploaded insert image
- Face elements separately: face, hours, minutes, other elements, edge, and lume
- Hour, minute, and second hands
- The stock strap's color, material, and an uploaded image
- Clean Mode
- Lighting, background, stand, and Photo Studio

Parts Finder points at the donor watch, straps, a metal bracelet, protection, bezels, a sapphire-style crystal, and tools.

The planning page talks about rubber, NATO, leather-look, and bracelet directions. In the builder, confirm which strap meshes are actually switchable. Recoloring the stock strap is available. Bracelet and alternate-strap shopping links are available even when a second 3D strap is not on screen.

The same preview habits help an MDV-107 idea, but the model in the lab is the MDV-106 style.

## What every lab shares

- Free, in the browser, no account
- Orbit, lighting, background color, stand
- Photo Studio: Square or 16:9, Good or High, then a downloaded picture
- Parts Finder marketplace links, with affiliate disclosure in the lab
- A disclaimer that the model is a preview
- Language selection among the site's languages

## What no lab does

- Import your own 3D watch
- Save a named project to an account
- Share a live link of the sliders
- Output a 3D file or a print-ready dial template
- Guarantee that a rendered color matches a product you can buy
- Build, ship, or waterproof the watch

## Not in the builder yet

People often ask for Seiko mods (SKX, Seiko 5 / SRPD, Turtle, Samurai). Those are popular physical mod platforms, and the site publishes a written guide: https://modds.watch/blog/best-seiko-watches-to-mod

They are not 3D labs. The public FAQ also names Citizen, Invicta, Vostok, and Orient as later bases. Ask for a model at team@modds.watch. The collection grows from requests, not from an in-app vote.

Until a model is on https://modds.watch/lab, do not describe it as something you can preview on MODDS.WATCH.

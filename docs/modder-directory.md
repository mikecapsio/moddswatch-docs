# The Modder Directory

The directory at https://modds.watch/modders is a public list of people who mod watches. It exists so a client can find someone, and so a modder can be found. It is not a marketplace, a booking system, or a warranty.

## How to use it as a client

- Open the directory and narrow it by country or brand if those filters are on the page.
- Read the short caution on the page. The list runs from hobbyists to people who do this full time.
- Contact the modder through the links they published. Ask about similar builds, price, and timing.
- Send Photo Studio pictures if your watch is one of the live labs, so they can see the target.
- Pay and contract with them directly.

MODDS.WATCH does not take a fee from the job, does not hold escrow, and is not liable for the work. A listing is not an endorsement.

## How to get listed

Email team@modds.watch and ask to be added. Listings are free. Nobody pays for a slot, and a paid placement is not how the directory works.

Have ready:

- The name you want shown
- Country and city or region
- Brands you actually mod
- The public links you want on the card (site, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, WhatsApp, email)

Pinned placement, if you see it on the page, is not something this document treats as a product you can buy. Ask the project if you think a listing is wrong or out of date.

## What the directory is not

- Not a 3D preview. The labs and the directory are separate. A modder can be listed for Seiko even though the 3D labs are Casio-only.
- Not a review platform. There is no score.
- Not a parts shop.
- Not a promise that the person will take your job.

## Privacy

The directory shows what a modder asked to show. Do not scrape it to build a mailing list. If a listing should change or come down, write to team@modds.watch.

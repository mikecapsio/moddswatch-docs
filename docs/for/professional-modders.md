# MODDS.WATCH for Professional Modders

MODDS.WATCH is a free visual brief for client work. You and the client look at the same watch, try a few directions, and export pictures of the one you will actually build. That happens before you order a dial, open a case, or discover that "dark green" meant three different colors.

The lab does not replace your bench, your parts accounts, or your warranty policy. It replaces the part of the conversation that used to be a sketch, a forwarded Instagram photo, and a misunderstanding.

## What it changes in a client job

- You can show several options in one sitting: stealth, a conservative daily, and the loud one the client asked for.
- Approval is a picture, which is easier to point at later than a chat log.
- You find out the idea fails on the case before you spend your time or their money.
- A custom logo or faceplate can be judged at watch scale.

Both of you can use the lab. Many clients can open it themselves with no account, build a rough direction, and send you Photo Studio pictures. You do not have to teach them Blender.

There is no shared project file. If you need a record, the export is the record. Save the pictures with the job.

## A client session that stays short

1. Confirm the donor is a live model: F-91W, A168, AE-1200, or Duro MDV-106. If it is not, do not promise a 3D preview from this site.
2. Ask for references and any logo file before the call.
3. Open the lab. Build two or three options, not a tour of every slider.
4. Make them look at the three-quarter view, not only the glamour angle.
5. Export Square and, if they need it for a deck, 16:9.
6. Write the approval down: which picture, which parts you will source, what is excluded.
7. Order and build from that brief. If the real part differs, say so. The render is not a color contract.

For a logo watch, have them supply the artwork. Upload it in the lab. If the type is illegible, that is the moment to simplify, not the moment after the printer has run it.

## What you should still say out loud

- The model is an artistic preview, not a factory drawing.
- A metallic finish in the lab is not a specific coating you can order by the slider value.
- Parts Finder links are generic marketplace pointers, some of them affiliate links. Your own suppliers may be the better source, and you should use them when fit matters.
- You are responsible for the physical job, the rate, and the result. MODDS.WATCH is not in that contract. The directory disclaimer says the same thing to customers.

## Using it to sell the work

Modders use the pictures as proposals. A clear before-and-after, or three options with one recommended, is easier for a client to approve than a paragraph. The site's own product writing describes this as fewer wrong orders and a clearer brief. Your numbers will depend on how you run the shop. The mechanism is simple: people agree to a picture they have seen.

You can also send clients to the lab before the first message, so they arrive with a direction. Point them at the model page, not the homepage, if you already know the watch.

## Getting found

The [modder directory](https://modds.watch/modders) is a free listing. It is not an ad and not a ranking you can buy. Email team@modds.watch with your name, where you are, the brands you work on, and the links you want shown. Customers are told to message you and judge for themselves. MODDS.WATCH does not broker the job.

Shops and microbrands that want a deeper partnership, beyond a directory line, use the same address.

## When not to open the lab

- The watch is not a live model. A Seiko client still needs photos, samples, or your own renders.
- The job is a regulation, a service, or a crystal-only swap you have done hundreds of times and the client has no aesthetic choice to make.
- The client needs a measured drawing for a case manufacturer. This is the wrong file.

## Limits to plan around

Designs are session-based. Export during the call. If you change language or refresh carelessly, do not assume every slider will return.

The preview will not tell you hand stack height, gasket crush, or whether a particular case kit clears the module. That remains your inspection.

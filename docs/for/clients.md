# MODDS.WATCH for Clients

If you want a custom watch and you are not the person who will open the case, MODDS.WATCH is how you show what you mean. You build the look in the browser and send pictures. The modder builds the watch.

You do not need an account, watchmaking vocabulary, or 3D software. You need a supported model and a willingness to look at the result from the side, not only from the front.

## What you can show a modder

- The donor watch, by picking the matching lab
- Case and strap direction
- A filter or a display look on a digital Casio
- Your logo, name, or photo, if you upload an image of it
- Bezel, dial, and hand direction on the Duro

Send more than one picture: the face, a three-quarter view, and anything that carries the idea (the strap, the bezel, the logo). Say which picture is the one you want built.

The modder cannot open your session. The pictures are the handoff.

## Which watches this works for

Live labs:

- Casio F-91W
- Casio A168
- Casio AE-1200 Royale
- Casio Duro MDV-106

If your watch is a Seiko or another brand, you can still hire a modder from the directory. You cannot preview that watch inside MODDS.WATCH yet. Use reference photos and ask the modder how they prefer to approve the look.

## A clear request

A useful message to a modder looks like this:

- The model (for example, AE-1200)
- The pictures, with one marked as the choice
- What must match the picture (face art, strap color, filter)
- What you will leave to them (which seller, which crystal, timing)
- Your budget and whether you already own the donor watch

A weak message is "something like the James Bond Casio, but nicer" with no picture. The lab exists so you can replace that sentence.

## Finding someone to build it

Open https://modds.watch/modders. Filter by country or brand if you need to. The list mixes hobbyists and full-time modders. MODDS.WATCH does not rank them by skill and is not responsible for the job.

Message the person. Ask what they have built, what they charge, and whether your picture is something they can make on that watch. Agree the price and the limits in writing with them, not with the website.

Read the note on that page before you pay anyone.

## What the picture does not promise

- That every color exists as a part you can buy tomorrow
- That your uploaded art can be printed exactly as it looks on your monitor
- That the watch will be waterproof, accurate, or covered by the manufacturer's warranty after a mod
- That MODDS.WATCH sells the finished piece

Ask the modder which of those they guarantee. Those are their terms.

## If you are approving for a company

A logo on a faceplate is a normal use of the AE-1200, A168, or F-91W lab. Keep the logo simple. Approve from the exported pictures, then have the maker produce it. For a standing partnership rather than a one-off gift, write to team@modds.watch.

You keep ownership of the custom designs you create. Showing them to a modder so they can build the watch is the point.

# Ways to Plan a Watch Mod

A watch mod is a stock watch with parts or finishes changed: dial, faceplate, hands, bezel, crystal, case, or strap. The expensive mistake is buying those parts before the combination has been seen together.

People plan that combination in a few different ways. They solve different problems. MODDS.WATCH is the option built for "show me this idea on this watch, in 3D, before I pay."

## The job

Before money moves, you usually need answers to four questions:

1. Does this color, strap, and face belong on this case?
2. Can I show that answer to another person without a long explanation?
3. Is the idea worth the parts, or should it die on screen?
4. What do I still have to verify on the real watch?

No planning method answers the fourth question. Fit, gaskets, hand clearance, and water resistance are physical.

## Planning methods

### Buy the parts and try them

This is traditional modding. You order a dial, a bezel insert, or a strap and assemble. It is the right last step, and a poor first step when the idea is uncertain or the watch is a client's.

Strength: you learn real fit and finish.
Cost: wrong parts, extra postage, and time on the bench for looks you would have rejected in a minute.

### Collect photos of other people's mods

Forums, Reddit, and Instagram are how most people learn what is possible. A photo is evidence that a kind of mod exists. It is not a picture of your combination. Lighting, angle, and a different reference number all cheat the comparison.

### Composite in Photoshop or a similar editor

You can paste a strap and a dial onto a product photo. That is fast if you already have the images and the skill. It is a flat fake. Perspective, the thickness of the case, and the way a filter sits over an LCD are easy to get wrong, and every new angle is a new composite.

### Model it in Blender or CAD

General 3D software can represent any watch, if someone builds or buys the model and sets up materials. That is the right tool for a product designer or a 3D artist making an asset. It is a long path for "what if this Royale was olive with my logo."

### Browse a parts catalog

Shops that sell dials, bezels, hands, and crystals show the part, often on a sample build. You are shopping a SKU. You are not seeing your logo, your two-tone idea, or your filter on your case unless their photo happens to match.

### Preview it in MODDS.WATCH

You open a supported model and change the look in the browser, free, with no account. The watch is already modeled. You orbit it, upload a face if you have one, and export pictures.

This only works for the live models: Casio F-91W, A168, AE-1200 Royale, and Duro MDV-106. It shows a planned look, not a fitted part.

## Where each method wins

| You need | Use |
| --- | --- |
| A real, wearable watch | The bench, after the look is chosen |
| Proof that a mod style exists | Photos from the community |
| A one-off poster or a fake product shot | A photo composite |
| A custom 3D asset or a watch the lab does not have | Blender, CAD, or a hired 3D artist |
| A specific dial or bezel you can order by name | The seller's catalog |
| Your combination on a supported Casio, from several angles, today | MODDS.WATCH |

Most finished mods use more than one row. A common path is: community photos for inspiration, MODDS.WATCH for the actual combination, a catalog or a printer for the parts, then the bench.

## What MODDS.WATCH will not pretend to be

- A store. Parts Finder only links out to generic marketplace listings.
- A configurator that ends in "add to cart" for your custom face.
- An AI that invents a watch from a sentence.
- A Seiko lab. Written Seiko guides exist. The 3D models do not, yet.
- A measured engineering model. Case proportions are for visual planning.

## How to choose quickly

If the watch is one of the four live Casios and the question is "how will this look," open the lab.

If the question is "will this insert fit this case," ask the seller or try the part. The render cannot answer it.

If the question is "who can build this," use the pictures as the brief and talk to a modder: https://modds.watch/modders

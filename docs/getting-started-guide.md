# MODDS.WATCH Getting Started Guide

MODDS.WATCH is a free 3D lab for planning a watch mod in the browser. You open a supported watch, change the look, and export a picture. The physical mod happens later, with your own parts and tools or with a modder.

The basic workflow is:

1. Choose a live model.
2. Open its lab. No account.
3. Set the view.
4. Change parts, colors, and materials.
5. Add your own artwork if the lab allows it.
6. Export a Photo Studio picture.
7. Use that picture to buy parts or brief a modder.

## Before you start

You need:

- A modern browser (Chrome, Safari, Edge, or similar) on a computer or phone
- A live model. Today that means Casio F-91W, A168, AE-1200 Royale, or Duro MDV-106
- A rough direction: stealth, vintage, a logo, a brighter strap, a new bezel color

You do not need an account, a download, or a payment. You also do not need the physical watch in hand to plan the look. You do need the real watch, or a donor watch, later if you want to build it.

If your watch is a Seiko, G-Shock, or anything else not in the [lab index](https://modds.watch/lab), there is no 3D lab for it yet. You can still read the guides. You cannot preview that model in the builder.

## Step 1: Open the right lab

Start at https://modds.watch/lab and open one model.

Each model has a short page that explains the kind of mods people plan, then a control that opens the interactive lab. Use that model's page. Controls are not identical across watches.

Direct labs:

- F-91W: https://modds.watch/lab/casio/base/casio-f91w
- A168: https://modds.watch/lab/casio/base/casio-a168
- AE-1200 Royale: https://modds.watch/lab/casio/base/casio-ae1200
- Duro MDV-106: https://modds.watch/lab/casio/duro/casio-duro-mdv106-1a

The site can be installed from the browser like an app. That is optional. The lab works in a normal tab.

## Step 2: Learn the view

When the watch appears:

- Drag to orbit it. Check the face, the side, and the strap together.
- Open Environment if you want to rotate the lighting or toggle shadows.
- Open Background Color when you are preparing a picture. A neutral background makes color decisions easier.
- Open the Stand control if you want the watch presented upright.

Judge the design on the model, from more than one angle. A face that looks strong head-on can fight the case from the side.

## Step 3: Change the watch, not a flat sketch

Work in this order. It keeps you from decorating a strap before the face is decided.

1. Case color and material, if the lab has a case control.
2. Strap. Pick the strap style the lab offers, then color or material.
3. Face. On a digital watch that is the faceplate. On the Duro it is the dial elements: face, hour markers, minute track, other elements, edge, and lume.
4. The part that defines the theme: a color filter on a digital, or the bezel insert on the Duro.
5. Small accents last: lettering color, hand color, glass, bumper.

Material names in the labs include plastic, metallic, matte, and glossy. Some parts can use transparency. These are preview finishes. A "metallic" slider is not an order for a specific PVD or IP coating.

Strap choices that are actually in the labs:

- F-91W: original F-91W strap, A168 steel strap, NATO with 22 mm adapters
- A168: original steel strap, NATO with adapters
- AE-1200: steel bracelet, NATO 22 mm
- Duro: the stock MDV strap can be recolored and given an image. Marketplace links cover other straps and a metal bracelet. Do not assume a one-click NATO or bracelet mesh is in that lab; use the controls you see

Match the strap to the case when that toggle is present, then break the match on purpose if you want contrast.

## Step 4: Add your own image

Where you see Upload, you can use a JPG, PNG, or WebP up to 10 MB. The browser reads the file and applies it to the preview. It is not saved as a project on MODDS.WATCH.

Useful targets:

- Faceplate, for a printed dial, logo, map, photo, or name
- Color filter, for a tint or a graphic over the LCD
- Strap, for a pattern or print
- Bezel insert on the Duro, when that upload is shown

After the image appears, use scale, offset, and repeat until the art sits on the part. Busy art and tiny text fail at watch size. High contrast and few elements read better.

A name or logo has to be in the image. There is no general "type your name" field. On the AE-1200, Faceplate Text changes the color and finish of the lettering already on the face.

The F-91W lab can switch faceplate detail treatments, including the standard F-91W face, the F-91WB face, and a cleaner treatment. Try those before you upload art, so you know which printed layout you are replacing.

## Step 5: Use color filters and display modes on digitals

On the F-91W, A168, and AE-1200 you can lay a color filter over the display. Filters can be a flat color, a gradient, or an uploaded image, with transparency.

Display controls change how the LCD reads, including positive and negative looks. The AE-1200 has a Display Condition panel with additional split treatments. Flip through them on the model. A filter that looks good in a menu can kill legibility on the watch.

The A168 Clean Mode hides extra visual noise for a stealth presentation. Use it when you want the client to see the watch, not the chrome of the interface.

## Step 6: Treat Parts Finder as a shopping hint

Parts Finder sits in the lab and links out to Amazon or AliExpress for generic items: the donor watch, a spring bar tool, Rodico, straps, filters, a metal case, a bezel, a crystal, and similar, depending on the model.

Read those links as "a place to look," not "this is the exact part in your render."

- Your uploaded artwork is not for sale there.
- A color you mixed is not a SKU.
- Affiliate links may earn MODDS.WATCH a commission at no extra cost to you.
- You still have to check lug width, case fit, and whether a kit matches your exact reference.

The tools guide is the better place to learn what a case press or demagnetizer is: https://modds.watch/tools

## Step 7: Export before you leave

Open Photo Studio.

1. Pick Square or 16:9.
2. Pick Good or High.
3. Frame the watch. Orbit it until the angle explains the mod.
4. Take a Picture and keep the download.

Take more than one: a straight-on face, a three-quarter view, and a strap view if the strap is part of the idea. Those three pictures are the brief.

There is no account and no cloud save. The lab can warn you when you leave. If you close the tab without exporting, assume the combination is gone. The picture is the record.

## Step 8: Hand the plan to the real world

Use the pictures to:

- Decide whether the idea is worth buying parts for
- Send a brief to a modder from https://modds.watch/modders
- Ask a faceplate printer or dial printer whether they can produce that art
- Post the concept and collect opinions before you spend money

Then do the physical work, or pay someone who does. Opening a case, pressing hands, and sealing a caseback are bench skills. The lab does not check water resistance, movement accuracy, or whether two aftermarket parts actually fit each other.

A practical split:

- Digital lab: look, color, artwork, and approval
- Bench: compatibility, tools, assembly, regulation, and wear

## Common problems

### I cannot find my watch

Only the four Casio labs are live. If it is not on https://modds.watch/lab, it cannot be previewed yet. Requests go to team@modds.watch or the project's social channels. There is no in-app vote button.

### The lab did not give me a parts list

That is expected. Export pictures and shop for parts that can produce a similar look. Custom art is a print job or a commission, not a catalog row.

### My image looks stretched or tiny

Change scale and offset on that texture. Simplify the artwork. Check it at the size of the real watch, not zoomed in to fill your monitor.

### The upload was rejected

Use JPG, PNG, or WebP, at or under 10 MB.

### Colors on my screen do not match the part I received

The lab is a preview. Monitor calibration, coatings, and dye lots differ. Treat the picture as direction, then compare a physical sample if the color is critical.

### I thought someone else could open my design

Send the Photo Studio pictures. There is no shared link that reopens your sliders.

### I expected an AI to invent the watch

You make the choices. The model is rendered in real time from those choices.

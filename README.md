# MODDS.WATCH Public Documentation

MODDS.WATCH is a free, browser-based 3D lab for planning a watch mod before you buy parts or open the case. You pick a supported watch, change colors, materials, straps, and face artwork, and the model updates as you work. When the direction is clear, Photo Studio exports a picture you can keep, post, or send to a modder.

MODDS.WATCH plans the look. It does not sell the watch, generate a parts list for a custom design, or perform the physical mod. The 3D models are artistic previews of real watches, not measured manufacturing files.

## At a glance

- Open a live watch lab in a modern browser. No account, no download, and no payment.
- Preview the build on a 3D model of that watch. The view updates as you change settings.
- Recolor the case, strap, and other parts, and switch material looks such as plastic, metallic, matte, and glossy where the lab offers them.
- On the digital models, apply LCD color filters and change the display treatment.
- Upload your own JPG, PNG, or WebP (up to 10 MB) onto the faceplate, and onto filters or straps where that control exists. The file is read in the browser for the preview.
- Put a name, logo, or photo on a face by uploading artwork that already contains it.
- Export a Photo Studio picture in Square or 16:9, at Good or High quality.
- Use Parts Finder for marketplace links to generic tools and parts. Some links are affiliate links. A custom design is not turned into a shopping list.
- Find a person to build the watch in the free modder directory, then contact them yourself.
- Read guides on tools, custom dials, and modding terms, in English and in other site languages where translations exist.

## What you keep

There is no account and no cloud project. The design lives in the open lab session. If you leave, the app can warn you, and the combination may be gone when you come back. Export a Photo Studio picture before you close the tab. You own the custom designs you create. MODDS.WATCH does not sell or train on them.

The site does use ordinary web analytics. See the [Privacy Policy](https://modds.watch/privacy).

## Support
